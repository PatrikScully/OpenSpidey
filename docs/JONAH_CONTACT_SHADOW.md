# Jonah contact and footprint

`JonahScorpionContact` ports the whole original `GoAroundScorp` routine at `0x446770`. The previous horizontal displacements remain in `JonahBrainState`; the contact composition owns only source desired/smoothed coordinates `0x33C/0x340/0x344/0x348`. Near Scorpion, the actor uses the original Q12 vector normalization to project a100-unit separation and smooth toward it. Far from Scorpion, the previous displacements decay independently and use the source8192 deadzone. All arithmetic and the64-unit adjustment rounding retain the original32-bit wrapping rules.

`tools/reference_jonah_contact_vectors.py` captures1,068 whole PC cases and2,370 source visits. The original contact, normalization and integer-conversion instructions execute unchanged; only the CRT square-root transport is supplied by the observer. Repeated visits remove the preceding displacement exactly as the outer AI does.

`JonahFootprintGeometry` implements `DoMGSShadow` (`0x446980`) with actual model hooks3/6/10/15. It reuses the previously proven source footprint projection, including the inverse body rotation, minimum X bounds±32 and Z bounds±64, ordered rotated corners and signed ground word at `0x222`. It does not use the generic scale-based `ActorShadowState`.

`tools/reference_jonah_shadow_vectors.py` captures400 whole original footprint cases, including original GTE math and source CQuadBit construction, list attachment, texture setup, transparency, subtraction flag and corner stores. The fresh resource path resolves the named Shadow2 override and allocates exactly one quad; repeated visits reuse it. Geometry is observed at the dynamic joint-hook transport, rather than substituting rendering or a synthetic model pose.

`GodotJonahFootprintShadow` owns one native protected quad and lazily creates its actual mesh, shader, material and Shadow2 texture on the first pose update. It receives the cached `BuildTransform` translation/rotation separately from the current body position, because procedures and contact can move the body after that source phase. Its ground plane reads the actual common script register0 at `0x222`. Source transparency64, depth bias32 and PC mix rendering are retained. Its shader is explicitly disposed with the other private resources. The native proof uses both genuine16-part,26-clip Jameson/Joel banks for312 real pose/orientation samples, a differing-current-position/cached-transform regression, real script register writes, exact decoded Shadow2 pixels, stable resource identities, reparenting and destruction.

The proof operates on private actual model and shadow owners. It does not register a partial Jonah actor or demonstrate complete L2A2 campaign traversal. Production still needs to bind contact and footprint updates to the genuine actor frame/body/effect owners and supply the world, actor, player, script and camera services required by the behavior compositions.
