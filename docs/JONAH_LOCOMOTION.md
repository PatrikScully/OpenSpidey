# Jonah movement, hiding and damage

`JonahLocomotionState` adds the waypoint role, obstruction counter, hit context, hiding-place handle, and next-room fields to the canonical `JonahBrainState`. It does not duplicate constructor state, actor signals, procedure completion, behavior flags, body motion, or animation. The existing path controller remains the source of path count and point storage through the required world host.

The concrete port includes the whole PC routines `FollowWaypoints` (`0x445330`), `Arrival` (`0x444840`), `GetNextWaypoint` (`0x444B70`), `LinkedHidingPlaceStillExists` (`0x445A90`), `TakeHit` (`0x4457B0`), `Hide` (`0x446130`), and `Die` (`0x445950`). `StartRunningToNextRoom` is inlined in the shipped arrival and recovery routines. `JonahDamage.Hit` ports the incoming damage entry at `0x4446E0`. Arrival's breakable-target message helper at `0x444590` is also concrete.

`JonahEncounterBehavior` retains one host adapter per actor and invokes the original outer AI ordering. Its movement, hiding, recovery, and death branches call the concrete helpers. The underlying services' old outer state-machine slots are never used; the managed tests throw if this composition reaches them. Complete initialization, idle, pleading, and panic remain in `JonahBrain`.

Waypoint queries read at most four source link records. The role and node type have different meanings: the first controls Jonah's behavior at arrival; the second identifies ordinary waypoint or environment records. Next-waypoint selection preserves the original single-link shortcut and random selection rules, including `Rnd(0)` when no ordinary multi-link candidate exists. Hiding-place checks clear only the source handle's pointer and preserve its validation word until a genuine environment body replaces the handle.

Movement consumes the shared procedure completion bit before parsing the current waypoint's script and applying arrival behavior. Its two animation modes use different movement speeds, turn rates, and facing tolerances. The PC's movement substate1 collision test passes a newly initialized zero destination; this behavior is retained and covered by the observer. Hiding checks the actual environment body identity against the player's held object, so destroying or picking up the hiding place affects the source decisions.

Incoming damage clears the standing signal and stores the hit context before the attack-kind6 early return. Health subtraction wraps as a signed16-bit value. A surviving hit accumulates obstruction damage unless the source protected flag is set, selects the actual recovery clip, and enters state6. A lethal hit selects the death clip and enters state8. The original Hit method returns0 throughout, including successful health changes; the port returnsfalse to preserve that contract.

Death consumes the actual animation-finished flag, clears Jonah's collision radius, then selects clip15 and applies the real player input-suppression timer. It directs the camera to Jonah with source mode3, zero offsets, horizontal distance75 and vertical distance-300. Subsequent visits turn the camera and accumulate the source wait timer. Reaching180 updates writes level status2. Camera absence preserves the state/timer behavior without camera calls.

`tools/reference_jonah_locomotion_vectors.py` captures2,800 whole original PC cases and69 canonical fields before each transport call. The corpus includes280 full AI visits with the concrete state leaves unpatched and280 incoming-hit→AI sequences, plus individual movement, waypoint, arrival, hiding, recovery, death, and incoming-hit observations. Original nested waypoint selection, hiding, arrival and recovery decisions execute inside the PE. Only the listed world, allocation, procedure-construction, animation, audio, player and camera transports are replaced. No complete actor movement or camera rendering is inferred from those transport stubs.

Production still needs a genuine Godot Jonah actor and the native host for source links, environment handles, path storage, authored waypoint scripts, movement/look procedures, Scorpion module/position queries, player state, audio, and camera/application services. `GoAroundScorpion` smoothing and shadow rendering remain separate required leaves. These Core ports and tests do not register a partial actor or claim playable L2A2 campaign completion.
